Healthcare is one of the few industries where the website is often the *first* clinical impression. Here is what we find breaking most often.

## 1. Booking is buried

If booking takes more than one tap from any page, you are losing patients who were ready.

## 2. No real photos

Stock photography of models in lab coats reads as a practice with something to hide. Photograph the actual team and the actual waiting room.

## 3. Insurance information is missing

"Do you take my insurance?" is the most common pre-visit question. Answer it on its own page.

## 4. Slow on mobile

Most patients arrive on a phone, often on cellular. A three-second delay is a measurable drop in bookings.

## 5. No local signals

Separate pages per location, consistent name/address/phone, and a maintained Google Business Profile.

## 6. Unreadable credentials

Board certifications and affiliations build trust — but only if a patient can find them.

## 7. No accessibility work

Beyond the legal exposure, accessible markup is what lets search engines and AI assistants read you properly in the first place.
