When someone asks ChatGPT for "the best dental practice in Austin," the answer does not come from a ranked list of ten blue links. It comes from a model summarising what it has read about you. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the work of making sure what it has read is accurate, current, and flattering.

## How GEO differs from SEO

SEO earns a position. GEO earns a mention. The two overlap, but they reward different things:

- **SEO** rewards relevance and authority against a query.
- **GEO** rewards content that is easy to quote, attribute, and verify.

An AI assistant will not cite a page it cannot parse into a clean, standalone claim. That changes how you write.

## Where to start

1. **Write citable claims.** Short, factual sentences that survive being lifted out of context.
2. **Add structured data.** Schema markup tells a model what kind of thing your page describes.
3. **Publish an `llms.txt`.** A plain-text summary of who you are and what you offer.
4. **Get mentioned elsewhere.** Models trust corroboration across sources more than your own claims.

## Measuring it

Traditional rank tracking will not show you any of this. Ask the assistants directly, on a schedule, and record whether you were named, how you were described, and which sources were cited.
