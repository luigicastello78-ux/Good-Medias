How We Work, Step-by-Step

A clear, proven roadmap from kickoff to compounding growth, with no guesswork and no surprises.

1
Discovery & AuditWeek 1–2
  • We dig into your business, customers, competitors and goals, then audit your current site for technical SEO issues, speed, conversion leaks and AI visibility.
  • You get a clear report showing where you rank today, what ChatGPT and Google say about your brand, and the fastest wins available.
2
Strategy & SitemapWeek 2–3
  • We build a keyword and topic map for your industry and turn it into a sitemap, where every page has a job: rank, convert or both.
  • Messaging, calls-to-action and structured data are planned upfront, so SEO and GEO are part of the foundation, not an afterthought.
3
Design & DevelopmentWeek 3–8
  • Our designers create conversion-focused, mobile-first designs you review and approve before a single line of code is written.

  • We launch with redirects, Search Console and analytics fully set up, then publish content, earn authority and grow AI citations month after month.
